# Analysis Summary

## Problem

A SaaS startup (GrowX Labs) in its early growth stage needs to optimize marketing performance, improve customer retention, and ensure sustainable revenue growth.

The key challenge is identifying which acquisition channels drive efficient growth while maintaining strong unit economics (CAC vs LTV).

---

## Approach

- Structured dataset into multiple layers (raw → processed → analysis)  
- Calculated CAC, LTV, MRR, and churn  
- Evaluated channel performance and revenue trends  
- Compared efficiency across acquisition channels  

---

## Key Findings

- Referral channels delivered the highest ROI with the lowest churn  
- Google Ads generated high revenue but showed inefficient spend  
- Revenue growth closely followed user acquisition trends  
- Growth was heavily dependent on marketing campaigns  
- CAC decreased while LTV increased, improving unit economics  

---

## Recommendations

- Reallocate budget toward high-ROI channels (Referrals)  
- Optimize or reduce inefficient ad spend (Google Ads)  
- Improve onboarding and retention strategies to reduce churn  
- Reduce dependency on campaign-driven growth  
- Focus on sustainable, long-term customer value  

---

## Outcome

The analysis highlights key growth drivers and inefficiencies, enabling data-driven decisions to improve profitability, optimize marketing spend, and strengthen long-term business sustainability.
